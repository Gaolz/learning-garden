# English for Technical Communication — Resources

## Knowledge

### Writing & Technical Communication

- [coderslingo.com](https://coderslingo.com/)
  Free, high-quality resource specifically for developers learning English. Covers technical documentation, design docs, blog post writing, and developer content creation. Use for: the go-to reference for any technical writing task. Articles tagged by level (Intermediate/Advanced).

- [Technical Writing Tips for Non-Native English Speakers — Fernando Paladini](https://paladini.dev/posts/technical-writing-tips-for-non-native-english-speakers/)
  Practical tips from a non-native speaker who writes technical content professionally. Covers mental barriers, drafting strategies, quote-search technique for verifying natural phrasing. Use for: motivation and workflow when starting to write.

- [English as a Second Language in Technical Writing — Gaurav Tiwari](https://gauravtiwari.org/english-as-a-second-language-esl-in-technical-writing/)
  Comprehensive guide for ESL developers writing technical content. Covers reading routines, phrase libraries, tools (Grammarly, Hemingway, AI assistants), and career benefits. Use for: building a sustainable writing practice and tool setup.

- [Google Developer Documentation Style Guide](https://developers.google.com/style)
  Free, authoritative reference for technical writing conventions. Covers word choice, tone, formatting, inclusive language. Use for: answering "how should I phrase this?" questions.

- [Microsoft Writing Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/)
  Free alternative to Google's guide. Covers similar ground with slightly different conventions. Use for: cross-referencing style decisions.

### Grammar & Mechanics

- [English Grammar in Use (5th Edition) — Raymond Murphy (Cambridge)](https://www.cambridge.org/cambridgeenglish/catalog/grammar-vocabulary-and-pronunciation/english-grammar-use-5th-edition)
  The gold standard for intermediate (B1-B2) self-study grammar. 142 units with clear explanations and practice exercises. Includes ebook with audio. Use for: targeted grammar reference when a specific pattern causes trouble — not for reading cover-to-cover.

### Tools

- **Grammarly** — catches article and preposition errors, the two hardest areas for Chinese-English learners. Premium explains the "why" behind corrections.
- **Hemingway Editor** — highlights complex sentences and suggests simplifications. Forces the simplicity that produces the best technical writing.
- **Google Docs** — built-in spell check is excellent for catching basic errors during drafting.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/EnglishLearning](https://reddit.com/r/EnglishLearning)
  Active, supportive subreddit for English learners. Use for: asking "does this sound natural?" questions, getting feedback on specific sentences.

- [HelloTalk](https://www.hellotalk.com/)
  Language exchange app: chat with native English speakers who want to learn Chinese. Text, voice, and video. Built-in translation and correction aids. Use for: finding regular speaking partners.

- [Hilokal](https://www.hilokal.com/en/speak/english)
  Free drop-in audio chatrooms for English speaking practice. Organized by level. Listen-first option for beginners. Use for: low-pressure speaking practice without scheduling.

## Gaps
- No structured curriculum for technical English speaking (as opposed to general conversation)
- Limited resources specifically for Chinese-English technical communication patterns
