# Mission: English for Technical Communication

## Why
I'm a developer who needs English to work in tech — reading documentation, collaborating with international teams, and building a professional presence. My spoken and written English currently limits my career reach. English proficiency means I can share my technical ideas with the global developer community and speak confidently with native speakers.

## Success looks like
- Writing and publishing a technical blog post in English that a native speaker could read without friction
- Holding a 15-minute unscripted conversation with a native speaker about a technical topic without getting stuck
- Reading technical documentation (e.g., library docs, RFCs) at speed without mental translation

## Constraints
- Currently at CET-4 level: can handle basic conversation and reading but struggles with production (speaking, writing)
- Self-study with AI tutor — no classroom or structured course
- Daily practice time: to be determined
- Learning must integrate with real work (tech/dev context)

## Out of scope
- Academic English or test prep (IELTS/TOEFL)
- Literary analysis, creative writing
- Advanced grammar theory for its own sake — only the grammar that improves communication
