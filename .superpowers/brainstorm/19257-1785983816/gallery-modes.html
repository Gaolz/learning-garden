Toggle Behavior

  • 📅 Month Grid — Default view. Shows all cards of current month. Prev/Next month buttons.
  • 📆 Year Heatmap — Toggle to see full year. Click any green cell → jump to that day's card.
  • 🏷️ Filter bar — Tag filter across both modes (e.g., click #nature → only nature photos in grid).
  • 🔍 Search — Full-text search across location, feeling_text, and who fields via Dataview.

Both modes live on Photo/INDEX.md. Month grid = default. Year heatmap = toggle tab.
